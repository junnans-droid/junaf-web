About JUNAF

Reshape human
expression.

JUNAF is defined by what it makes perceptible — not by a single medium.

Founded by artist and creator Son Ye, JUNAF works across original sound, visual art and emerging forms of experience. Technology is a material, never the identity.

Every work begins with one question: if we invented this again today, would it still take the same form?
